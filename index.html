About Me

I am a PhD research scholar at IIT Roorkee, working in intelligent control and autonomous aerial robotics. My research interests include AI-based control systems, autonomous UAV navigation, dynamic obstacle avoidance, and optimization algorithms.

My work lies at the intersection of control systems, robotics, artificial intelligence, and optimization, with the goal of developing reliable algorithms for autonomous quadrotor UAVs operating in dynamic and uncertain environments.

Research & Projects

  • Autonomous UAV Navigation: Developing navigation and obstacle-avoidance strategies for dynamic environments.
  • Intelligent Control: Investigating AI-based control approaches for aerial robotic systems.
