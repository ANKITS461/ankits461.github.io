About Me

I am a PhD research scholar at IIT Roorkee, working in the area of Intelligent Control and Autonomous Aerial Robotics, with a particular focus on AI-based control systems, autonomous UAV navigation, and dynamic obstacle avoidance. My academic interests lie at the intersection of control systems, robotics, artificial intelligence, and optimization algorithms. My background and coursework have provided me with an understanding of mathematical modelling, system dynamics, state-space representation, stability analysis, robot kinematics and dynamics, and intelligent control techniques such as fuzzy logic, neural networks, and adaptive control. My current research focuses on autonomous drone navigation and dynamic obstacle avoidance. I am particularly interested in developing and evaluating algorithms that enable quadrotor UAVs to navigate safely in environments containing static and moving obstacles while maintaining trajectory tracking and system stability.
